---
title: What this tool cannot see
rules: general
---

## Things outside its view
- It looks only at the transfers it was given. It does not know who owns an account, what a payment was for, or what happened in other systems.
- It looks at short windows of time. A slow pattern spread over days or weeks is not detected by these rules.
- It sees amounts and timing only. It has no information about devices, locations or identity documents.

## Patterns it does not look for
- Many small transfers kept just under a reporting limit.
- Activity that mixes cash, cards or other channels not present in the data.
- Relationships that only show up across a very long history.

## What that means for reviewers
- A quiet alert list does not mean nothing happened. It means nothing matched these four patterns in the data provided.
- A new alert is a prompt to look, not a finding. Reviewers bring the context the tool lacks.
- All results in this project come from synthetic data, so they illustrate how the method works and say nothing reliable about real customers.
